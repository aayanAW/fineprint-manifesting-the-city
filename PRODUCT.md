# PRODUCT.md — FinePrint ("Manifesting the City")

register: product

## Product Purpose

Enter a NYC building, see its real Local Law 97 carbon fine for every compliance period, the 2030 cliff, the cost-optimal retrofit plan that drives the fine toward $0, and the citywide picture. A deterministic engine computes every number; AI only ranks, explains, narrates, and cites.

## Users

Building owners, co-op boards, property managers, sustainability consultants, and hackathon judges (academics from CMU, CalTech, Columbia, Stanford) evaluating real-world impact, equity, and genuine use of AI.

## Brand & Tone

"The Ledger": editorial fine-print aesthetic. Newspaper masthead, legal document, financial ledger. Authoritative, precise, honest. Every number is a labeled estimate with provenance; the interface itself reads like the fine print it decodes. Danger is typographic (weight, hatching, scale), never decorative color.

## Strategic Principles

1. Code computes every number; the LLM never does arithmetic. The UI must make this boundary visible (provenance footnotes, "computed by engine" labels).
2. Honesty over polish: nulls render as "unknown," caveats render verbatim.
3. The 2030 cliff is the headline of every building story.
4. Equity is first-class: Article 321 / affordable pathway is presented with dignity, not as an afterthought.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards (gradient hero metrics, identical card grids, glassmorphism), crypto-neon climate dashboards, Bloomberg-terminal cosplay, anything that hides uncertainty.

## Scope notes

No auth, no accounts. Frontend-first with mock data typed to the locked backend contract; real engine/data wiring follows.
